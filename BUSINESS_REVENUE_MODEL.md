---

### **Tier 1: B2C Freemium & Pro Farmer Subscription (B2C SaaS)**
- **Free Tier (Zero Cost)**: Unlimited farm location setup, live weather from Open-Meteo, soil texture classification, 5 crop evaluations, and basic recommendations. (Builds massive user acquisition & farmer trust).
- **AgroNex Pro (₹199 / month or ₹1,499 / year)**:
  - Unlimited What-If sensitivity simulations.
  - Hyper-local Mandi price surge and weather risk alerts via WhatsApp/SMS.
  - Custom soil lab PDF report auto-parsing.
  - Harvest schedule optimization.

---

### **Tier 2: Agri-Input & Equipment Marketplace (B2B Commission)**
- Farmers who select a recommended crop (e.g. *Tomato*) require seeds, drip irrigation kits, organic pesticides, and soil amendments.
- AgroNex partners with verified seed companies, bio-pesticide manufacturers, and drip irrigation distributors (e.g. Jain Irrigation, Netafim, UPL).
- **Monetization**: **5% to 10% referral commission** on seed/fertilizer orders placed through the app or sponsored input recommendations.

---

### **Tier 3: Agri-Fintech Credit Scoring & Risk API for Banks & Insurance (B2B Enterprise)**
- Rural banks (e.g. SBI Agri, NABARD), micro-finance institutions (MFIs), and crop insurance providers lose millions on defaulted Kisan Credit Card (KCC) loans due to opaque crop risk assessment.
- **AgroNex Risk Score API**: Banks query AgroNex API (`/api/enterprise/credit-score`) to get an objective agronomic risk severity score (0–100) before disbursing loans.
- **Monetization**:
  - **Pay-Per-Query**: ₹50 per loan risk check.
  - **Enterprise License**: ₹25,000 / month for regional banking branches.

---

### **Tier 4: Government & Agri-Research Data Licensing (B2G Enterprise)**
- State Departments of Agriculture and Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs) require aggregated, anonymized regional data on crop adoption trends and regional drought vulnerability.
- **Monetization**: B2G enterprise analytics dashboard licensing (₹5 Lakhs / year per district).
